About Us

We Build Where Hardware Meets Software

Tech-3ree is a research-based startup. We believe the most beautiful and worthwhile technology for society is born when hardware and software are designed as one system.

Our Belief

Software is Nothing without Hardware

This is not a tagline. It is the reason every product we make requires significant hardware skills — sensor and PCB design, quantum control electronics, optical engineering — engineered in lockstep with the software layer.

🔬

Research-First

We do not resell commodity devices. We run experiments, build prototypes, and validate with hospitals and labs before claiming a product is ready.

🛠️

Hardware-Deep

Every product starts at the physical layer: circuit boards, sensors, optical paths, and quantum control systems. Software is written to fit the hardware, not the other way around.

🌍

Society-Facing

Our work targets problems that matter: cardiovascular deaths, broken drug pipelines, and the next human-computer interface. Technology must be worthwhile.

Our Vision

Hardware-Integrated Research for Health, Computation, and Display

Wrist-side cardiology today. Quantum-assisted therapeutics tomorrow. Volumetric interfaces the day after.

We see a future where the best medical devices are not imported full-body suits but locally engineered, wrist-sized systems that understand the patient populations they serve. Where drug discovery is not bottlenecked by classical compute. And where information is no longer confined to flat screens.

Our Story

From Prototype to Platform

Research Begins

Identified Pakistan's cardiovascular monitoring gap and began building a wrist-wearable that meets hospital-grade standards.

Hospital Validation

Engaged with Shifa International Hospital's Biomedical Engineering department, who confirmed demand for a wrist-form SpO₂ and NIBP solution.

Quantum R&D

Launched the hybrid QNN drug-discovery research line as a Final Year Project, exploring qubit-based molecular representation.

Holography Research

Started development of a miniature water-tank hologram to bring true volumetric displays out of the laboratory.

Next: Incubation & Pilot Deployment

Seeking support to refine the cardiovascular monitor, secure DRAP certification, and deploy pilots in Lahore and Karachi.
