Product 02 — Computation

Hybrid Quantum Neural Network for Drug Discovery

Qubits and entanglement run the molecular-representation layer of a hybrid quantum-classical model, while a classical optimiser drives training and output.

  • 1

    Quantum Representation

    Qubits encode molecular structures in a high-dimensional state space that classical bits cannot efficiently represent.

  • 2

    Classical Optimisation

    A classical optimiser handles training; the quantum layer handles the parts of the computation where qubits deliver genuine advantage.

  • 3

    Pre-Validated Candidates

    The model produces a ranked candidate set with confidence scores, formatted for direct hand-off into wet-lab assays.

⚛️

Research Roadmap

Currently being developed as a Final Year Project, with a licensable model architecture that any pharma partner or research lab can use.

Product 03 — Display

Miniature Underwater Hologram

A compact water-tank display that renders true volumetric imagery through programmable light interference — viewable by several observers at once, without glasses or headsets.

  • 1

    True Volumetric Projection

    Engineered interference patterns reconstruct real 3D volumes inside a controlled water tank.

  • 2

    Compact Form Factor

    The water column acts as part of the optical path, shrinking the device to a desktop or benchtop unit.

  • 3

    Programmable Content

    Update content frame-by-frame — medical imaging, scientific visualisation, and interactive 3D content from one render engine.
